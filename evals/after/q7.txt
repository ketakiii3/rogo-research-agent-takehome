### Takeaways
- **Acme Robotics is by far the fastest-growing** of the three, more than quadrupling revenue from $210M to $1,015M over four years, with growth accelerating each year (45–49% YoY in recent years) [financials: Acme Robotics FY2021–FY2025].
- **Acme Corp** has grown steadily at a mid-single-digit pace (~5–6% annually), with revenue rising from $1,840M to $2,260M [financials: Acme Corp FY2021–FY2025].
- **Globex** is the largest by revenue (~$8.9B vs. Acme Corp's $2.3B and Acme Robotics' $1.0B) but the slowest grower, with revenue actually dipping in FY2023 ($8,395M) before recovering [financials: Globex Inc FY2021–FY2025]. Its 4-year CAGR of ~1.4% is well below both Acme entities.

### Profitability trend alongside growth
- Acme Corp's gross margin has expanded modestly, from 38.1% (FY2021) to 39.7% (FY2025) [financials: Acme Corp FY2021, FY2025].
- Acme Robotics has higher and also expanding gross margins, from 51.4% to 53.0% [financials: Acme Robotics FY2021, FY2025].
- Globex has the lowest gross margin of the three, rising from 29.2% to 30.8% [financials: Globex Inc FY2021, FY2025].
