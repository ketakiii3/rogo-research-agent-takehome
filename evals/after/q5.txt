## GLBX vs. ITCH: Business Quality Comparison

These are two very different kinds of businesses — GLBX is an $8.9B diversified industrial conglomerate, while ITCH is a ~$1.1B enterprise software company — so which one is "better" really depends on what you're optimizing for. Here's how they stack up across the key dimensions.

### Growth

| Metric | GLBX | ITCH |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue CAGR (FY21–FY25/24) | ~1.4% (FY21 $8,420M → FY25 $8,905M) [financials: Globex Inc FY2021, FY2025] | ~15.6% (FY21 $640M → FY24 $988M) [financials: Initech FY2021, FY2024] |
| Latest annual growth | +2.1% in FY2025, but only **0.6% organic** — the rest came from two bolt-on acquisitions [DOC-GLBX-001] | +14.8% in FY2024 [DOC-ITCH-002]; FY2025 preliminary guidance of **+13–15%** [DOC-ITCH-001] |
| 3-yr trend commentary | Management says growth has averaged ~1.9%/year, reflecting "mature end markets" and FX drag; the capital allocation priority is margin expansion, not growth [DOC-GLBX-002] | Third straight year of low-to-mid-teens growth, driven by the subscription transition and vendor consolidation among mid-market financial institutions [DOC-ITCH-002] |

ITCH is growing roughly 10x faster than GLBX, and nearly all of GLBX's growth is acquired rather than organic.

### Profitability

| Metric | GLBX FY2025 | ITCH FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | 30.8% [financials: Globex Inc FY2025] | 74.8% [financials: Initech FY2024] |
| Operating margin | 9.9% ($878M/$8,905M) [financials: Globex Inc FY2025] | 17.5% ($173.1M/$988M) [financials: Initech FY2024] |
| Net margin | 7.3% ($649.7M/$8,905M) [financials: Globex Inc FY2025] | 13.0% ($128.1M/$988M) [financials: Initech FY2024] |
| FCF margin | 6.0% ($535.6M/$8,905M) [financials: Globex Inc FY2025] | 10.7% ($105.6M/$988M) [financials: Initech FY2024] |

ITCH converts revenue to cash and profit far more efficiently — consistent with a software model, versus GLBX's industrial/manufacturing base (energy equipment, building products, specialty chemicals).

### Trajectory / margin direction

GLBX's gross margin has been slowly expanding — from 29.2% in FY2021 to 30.8% in FY2025 [financials: Globex Inc FY2021, FY2025] — consistent with its stated strategy of prioritizing margin expansion and portfolio simplification. That's included closing 11 manufacturing facilities since FY2022, with plans to divest at least one non-core business in FY2026 [DOC-GLBX-002].

ITCH's margins have also crept up, with gross margin rising from 73.6% to 74.8% between FY21 and FY24 [financials: Initech FY2021, FY2024], as the mix shifts toward higher-margin subscription revenue — 62% of FY2024 revenue, expected to rise to 68% in FY2025 [DOC-ITCH-001].

### Key risks / caveats

- **ITCH**: FY2025 figures are preliminary and unaudited. Initech has flagged that its 10-K will be delayed while it reviews revenue recognition on a set of Q4 multi-year contracts, and final results "may differ materially" from the guided $1.12–1.14B range [DOC-ITCH-001]. Management also flags the conversion pace of remaining perpetual-license customers as the main risk to the subscription transition [DOC-ITCH-002].
- **GLBX**: Organic growth is very weak (0.6% in FY2025), and reported growth leans heavily on acquisitions. Management itself describes end markets as "mature" with FX headwinds on its ~48% non-North America revenue base [DOC-GLBX-001], [DOC-GLBX-002].

### Bottom line

On growth, margins, and cash conversion, **ITCH looks like the structurally better business** — much faster growth, far higher margins, and a subscription-driven mix shift that's improving unit economics further. **GLBX is the larger, more diversified, and currently more stable** business, but it's growing organically at well under 1% and relies on M&A and cost/footprint actions for margin gains.

The main offset is risk: ITCH's FY2025 numbers are preliminary, with an active revenue-recognition review and a delayed 10-K — a real near-term red flag that GLBX doesn't have. If that review resolves cleanly, ITCH is the stronger business on fundamentals; if it doesn't, this assessment could change.
